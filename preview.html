    ABOUT AFTERGLOW

    A place to name it.

    Choose a feeling, add the context you want to remember, and come back to it later. Your choices shape the colors on the screen.

    The vocabulary draws on emotion-labeling research. The palette is expressive, not a scientific scale. No feeling is scored as good or bad.

    This prototype is a reflective journal, not a diagnostic tool or a mental health treatment. It does not provide AI advice or monitor entries.

    Research background: Putting feelings into words (2007).
