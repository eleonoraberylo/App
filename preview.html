ABOUT AFTERGLOW

A place to name it.

Choose a feeling, add the context you want to remember, and come back to it later. Your choices shape the colors on the screen.

Use your own words. The palette is expressive, not a scientific scale. No feeling is scored as good or bad.

This prototype is a reflective journal, not a diagnostic tool or a mental health treatment. Reflection questions are prewritten journal prompts. Entries are processed in this browser and are not sent to an AI service. Entries are not monitored.

Research background: Putting feelings into words (2007).

Suggested activities are prewritten and selected by the feeling words you enter. Your context is saved with your moment; it is not interpreted by AI.
